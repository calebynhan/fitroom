Try-ons

How try-ons work

A try-on is your own full-body photo, re-dressed in UNIQLO pieces: only the clothes change, so the face, body and background stay yours.

What counts as a try-on

  • A new outfit on your photo, or the same outfit with one piece changed, is one try-on.
  • Trying the same outfit again, or on your side photo, is free.
  • A try-on that makes no photo (the model was busy, you cancelled) is given back.

How many you have

Every FitRoom account (sign in with Google) gets free try-ons.

More try-ons are available on the FitRoom website, for the same account. They count wherever you use FitRoom, including in ChatGPT.

In ChatGPT

Connect FitRoom in ChatGPT with the same Google account and your try-ons are there too: each photo FitRoom makes there uses one. Nothing is sold in ChatGPT; when an account has no try-ons left, FitRoom says so. Size advice is always free.

One more limit

On the website, try-ons run on Hugging Face's free GPUs, which allow a few a day per connection, whatever your FitRoom balance. When they're used up for the day, try again tomorrow.
