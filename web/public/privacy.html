Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. Applies to the FitRoom website and the FitRoom plugin for ChatGPT.

Your photos

  • FitRoom never stores or logs your photos. On the website they stay in your browser tab and are gone when you close it.
  • To make a try-on, your photo goes from your browser to the FASHN VTON try-on model on Hugging Face (a public demo run by FASHN), which keeps uploads on its server for a while; FitRoom doesn't control that.
  • In ChatGPT, the photo you attach is held by ChatGPT (OpenAI) under its terms. For a try-on, FitRoom's server downloads it into memory, sends it to FASHN's try-on API, which keeps no copy of the image after the request, and returns the result to the FitRoom card in your chat. FitRoom doesn't store or log it.
  • Only use photos of yourself.

What FitRoom keeps

  • Your account (only if you sign in): your Google account id and email address, how many free try-ons you've used, how many you've bought, and a fingerprint of each look charged (a one-way digest of the photo and outfit, never the photo). Purpose: counting your try-ons, the same on the website and in ChatGPT.
  • Purchases: the Stripe checkout session id of each purchase, so it is credited once. Stripe handles payment; FitRoom never sees card details.
  • Sign-in to ChatGPT: when you connect FitRoom in ChatGPT, FitRoom issues ChatGPT signed tokens that name your account. They are not stored by FitRoom; they expire (an hour; refresh tokens 60 days).
  • In your browser only: your measurements, fit preferences, saved outfits and a count of today's free GPU use (localStorage). FitRoom's servers never receive your measurements, except the ones you give ChatGPT for a size, which are used for that answer and not kept.
  • ChatGPT without a FitRoom account (when try-ons there are free): a count of try-on photos per month, kept under a one-way digest of ChatGPT's anonymous user id.
  • Plugin usage counts: for each request ChatGPT makes to FitRoom, which tool ran, how it ended (for example "ok" or "error"), how long it took and which product it was about; and a count of distinct users per day, kept in a form that can count people but not list them (built from the same one-way digest). Never measurements, sizes, photos or what you wrote.
  • Fit reports you give in ChatGPT ("the M was tight in the chest"): added to anonymous tallies per item, size and verdict, which FitRoom shows other shoppers next to the size advice. To keep one report per person per item, FitRoom keeps a one-way digest of who reported which item.
  • Analytics: anonymous page views without cookies, and counts of try-on outcomes and checkout steps (Vercel Web Analytics). Never photos, emails or outfits.

Who receives data

  • Hugging Face and FASHN: your photo and the garment photo, for the try-on (on the website, FASHN's public demo on Hugging Face; in ChatGPT, FASHN's API).
  • Google: sign-in. Stripe: payments. Vercel and Upstash: hosting and the account store.
  • OpenAI: when you use FitRoom in ChatGPT (the conversation and attachments are ChatGPT's), and, only if the paid photo generator is switched on, your photos to make the generated shots.
  • FitRoom doesn't sell data or use it for advertising.

How long

  • Photos: not kept by FitRoom.
  • Look fingerprints: 90 days. Purchase records: about 13 months. Your account counts: while your account exists.
  • Plugin usage counts and daily user counts: 90 days. Fit-report tallies: up to 2 years; the one-report-per-item check: 180 days.

Your controls

  • Sign out on the website at any time; disconnect FitRoom in ChatGPT's settings.
  • Clear your measurements and saved outfits by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • To get a copy of your account data or have it deleted, contact FitRoom (below).
  • Usage counts and fit-report tallies don't name anyone, so FitRoom can't find or delete one person's share of them.

Contact

Questions, data requests and deletion: open an issue on FitRoom's GitHub.
