Privacy

Effective 11 November 2026. This page covers the FitRoom website (fitroom-woad.vercel.app) and FitRoom in ChatGPT (the plugin).

The short version. FitRoom doesn't store your photos or your measurements. It has no accounts. What it keeps is anonymous: counts of how the plugin is used, and tallies of how sizes fit ("3 of 5 buyers say the S fits"). Your photos go only to the try-on service that dresses them, and only when you ask for a try-on.

On the website

  • Measurements, fit preferences and saved outfits stay in your browser (local storage) and never reach FitRoom's servers.
  • Photos stay in the browser tab's memory and are never stored by FitRoom. When you ask for a try-on, your photo goes from your browser straight to the free FASHN VTON try-on model on Hugging Face (operated by FASHN), which keeps uploads in its cache for a while. That cache is outside FitRoom's control. Closing the tab removes the photo from FitRoom's page.
  • Pasted product links go to FitRoom's server, which loads that product from UNIQLO.
  • Page analytics use Vercel Web Analytics: page views without cookies, with addresses stripped of anything after "?" or "#". Each try-on photo sends one event: the outcome (for example "ok" or "busy"), which photo (front or side), how many garments, how many earlier results were reused, and how long it took. Never photos or outfits.
  • An optional paid photo generator (off unless the site says otherwise) sends your photos through FitRoom's server to OpenAI to make the pictures. FitRoom doesn't store them. A signed cookie counts how many looks you've made, and your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit requests.

In ChatGPT

When ChatGPT uses FitRoom, it sends FitRoom what the tool needs: the product, the measurements you shared, your fit preference and, for a try-on, a link to the photo you attached. It also sends anonymous IDs for you and for the conversation, which OpenAI creates and FitRoom can't trace back to you. OpenAI keeps your conversation under its own privacy policy.

Who else handles data

  • Vercel hosts FitRoom. Its logs hold request records, errors and the usage events above, never photos or measurements.
  • Upstash (when enabled) stores the anonymous counters and tallies.
  • FASHN runs the try-on: the API for ChatGPT, and the free Hugging Face model for the website. See FASHN's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI runs ChatGPT and the optional paid photo generator.
  • UNIQLO provides product data, and every item links to uniqlo.com. Anything you buy, you buy from UNIQLO under its own policies.

Your choices

  • Size advice works without a photo. Try-on is always something you ask for.
  • To clear the website's data, clear this site's storage in your browser.
  • FitRoom holds nothing that names you. That means it can't find or delete one person's anonymous counts or fit reports.

Children

FitRoom is not meant for anyone under 13. Never use a photo of a child.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests: open an issue on FitRoom's GitHub.
