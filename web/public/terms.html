Size advice is guidance

Sizes come from UNIQLO's size charts. Any measurement you leave blank is estimated from your height and weight, and the advice says when that happens. Garments vary, and people like different fits, so FitRoom can't promise a size will fit. Check UNIQLO's size guide and return policy before you buy.

Try-on photos

A try-on photo is made by an AI model. It shows the garment as UNIQLO photographed it, not in your size, and it can get details wrong. Use it to judge colour and style, and the fit card for size.
